# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Focus criticism on ideas and code, accept
responsibility for mistakes, and prioritize the project's public-interest mapping goals.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and unwanted sexual attention are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate. Report conduct concerns
through the repository maintainer's private GitHub contact channel. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and kept confidential as far as practical.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
